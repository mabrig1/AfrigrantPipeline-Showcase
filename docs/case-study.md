# Engineering case study: AfrigrantPipeline

## Problem
Applicants need a structured way to discover funding opportunities and understand eligibility without treating a recommendation as an award guarantee.

## Approach
Separate eligibility screening from ranking. Preserve source provenance, distinguish missing information from confirmed eligibility, and direct applicants to official criteria for final review.

## Evidence
The independent example evaluates fictional opportunities using synthetic education-level and subject fields. It does not retrieve real scholarships, process CVs, call AI providers or establish production functionality. No funding outcomes or performance metrics are claimed.

## Interview topics
Data normalization, explainable filtering, uncertainty, privacy, source verification and automated testing.
